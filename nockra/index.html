HOW NOCKRA WORKS

Readable before it is powerful.

Every tool follows the same transaction discipline.

01

Load the contract

Enter a token, pool or position. Nockra reads the live state first.

02

Review the action

Amounts, addresses, permissions and protocol targets remain visible.

03

Simulate where possible

Known reverts are surfaced before a state-changing transaction is sent.

04

Sign and confirm

Success appears only after the transaction receipt confirms on-chain.
